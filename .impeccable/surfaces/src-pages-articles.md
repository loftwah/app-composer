## Direction contract

**Thesis.** Tell one concrete maker story per article and show the proof at the point it matters.

**Own-world.** Use the existing App Composer shell and typography, restrained rules, and full-size commissioned illustration. The real screenshots carry their own product context.

**Story.** Readers understand the input, the decision, and what actually worked. Each story links to a useful next action at the end.

**First viewport.** A short route back, a substantive headline and a one-paragraph lead sit above one wide image. On a phone the same order fits without horizontal scroll.

**Form.** A reading page with a narrow body and a wide image field. seed: none (unavailable).

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
