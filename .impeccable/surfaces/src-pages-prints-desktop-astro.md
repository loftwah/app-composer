# Prints Desktop

The product home is prints.loftwah.com. App Composer retains its project showcase.

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** A creative archive makes the choices behind a finished piece
visible. Lead with the work and offer a direct path to keeping an original.

**Own world.** Space Grotesk and the native aperture anchor the brand.
The mounting paper and image mat draw from Afterlight. Published portfolio
work keeps its own lettering and colours.

**Story.** Browse the archive, hang a print, see the same workflows in the
native app, then download or open the practical guide.

**First viewport.** Pair the product promise and download with a large
ringside still and two supporting prints. Credit each work at the point of
viewing. Put the first-project tutorial and launch story next to the preview.

**Form.** Code-led extension with source-linked web renditions of bundled
originals. Native captures prove the workflow. The ImageGen darkroom
campaign card links back from Share to the same showcase.

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
