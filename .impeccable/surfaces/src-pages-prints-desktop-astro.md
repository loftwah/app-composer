# Prints Desktop

The product home is prints.loftwah.com. App Composer retains its project showcase.

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** Show the work behind the work. A music release, a game campaign
and a real play session each leave a different kind of print.

**Own world.** Space Grotesk and the native aperture anchor the brand.
The mounting paper and image mat draw from Afterlight. Published portfolio
work keeps its own lettering and colours.

**Story.** Browse the archive, hang a print, see the same workflows in the
native app, then download or open the practical guide.

**First viewport.** The question of what creators keep sits beside four
proofs from the Loftwah archive. Download and Afterlight entry points
remain visible, and the site navigation fits on a phone.

**Form.** Code-led extension with identical originals in the native app
and source-linked web renditions. Native captures explain the actual build.

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
