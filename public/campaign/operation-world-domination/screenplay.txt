# App Composer: Operation World Domination

An original miniature spy comedy. Orson Quill, a meticulous self-appointed mastermind, presents from a brass underwater operations room. Mabel Finch, his unflappable assistant, has read the appendices. A red lever, a brass key and a very small shark recur. The fictional briefing illustrates the roadmap. Direct captures are identified as current product.

## Cast

**Orson Quill**. British-inflected baritone. Tightly wound, delighted when explaining logistics. Treat a small inconvenience as a state secret. Bravado deflates whenever Mabel asks about ownership.

**Mabel Finch**. Quiet precise alto. Dry, almost conversational. The truth sounds like a correction to the minutes. Never plays the punchline.

## Cold open

INT. UNDERSEA BRIEFING ROOM, NIGHT. Empty chair. Red lever. A brass key drops onto the conference table. Quill comes out of the teal shadows. Finch looks up from her minutes exactly once.

**Orson Quill**

Miss Finch. Dim the lights. I'm about to take over the world.

**Mabel Finch**

They're already dim. We share a meter with the aquarium.

**Orson Quill**

Very well. Our target is one. Trillion. Tokens.

**Mabel Finch**

And what are we holding the world to ransom with?

**Orson Quill**

Useful software.

**Mabel Finch**

I'll get a smaller siren.

## Operation Frickin’ Easy

Finch tries the intimidating button. Instead of an alarm, a little lamp lights. Cut to live Learn and the real public starter. An illustrative brass key passes from Quill to an unseen owner.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Frickin' Easy. Start with the app you wish existed. Get Ready takes you through setup with an agent you can use.

**Orson Quill**

The mission is your first useful app, live in your own Cloudflare account. Your own source. And a second change you can come back and make.

**Mabel Finch**

Who keeps the keys?

**Orson Quill**

They do.

**Mabel Finch**

You had keys cut for everybody?

**Orson Quill**

I got a very nasty bulk discount.

## Operation Mini-Me

INT. SWITCHBOARD. Copper patch cords click into a console. Quill points to an empty chair reserved for the chosen agent. Finch stamps APPROVAL REQUIRED on the file.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Mini-Me. Bring your own agent. Em Dash is the command centre. Discord is the line to the lair. M C P gives the agent scoped tools, and GitHub keeps the source and the record.

**Orson Quill**

The goal is bounded autonomous delivery. An agent can carry a change through review, release and verification when authorised.

**Mabel Finch**

And the human can say no?

**Orson Quill**

That's why the veto button is bigger than mine.

## Operation LEGO Empire

INT. MINIATURE WORKSHOP. A walnut bench with abstract modular building blocks, no branded toy forms. A guestbook fits next to a link hub. Finch lets Quill discover the label on the third drawer.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Lego Empire. Reuse independent apps, widgets, infrastructure and agent skills. A guestbook and a link hub can work together and still keep their own data and release cycles.

**Mabel Finch**

What if the piece I need doesn't exist?

**Orson Quill**

Build the piece. We are an evil empire of very clearly labelled drawers.

**Mabel Finch**

This one says spare buttons.

## Operation Hollywood

INT. MINIATURE SOUNDSTAGE. A spotlight warms up. Clapboard, analogue synth, original figurine. Cut to a public Media Studio screen and an approved live playable demo. Finch slowly pushes a script away from Quill.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Hollywood. The ambition is films, music, games and characters you can keep working on. Media Studio already brings images, sound and video into one project.

**Orson Quill**

Save the takes. Keep the editable work. Give the soundtrack a proper ending.

**Mabel Finch**

Do I get a speaking part?

**Orson Quill**

You have seventeen.

**Mabel Finch**

That's how they get you.

## Operation Unlimited Tokens

INT. GENERATOR ROOM. Two physical meters. One stops when the job is done. The other wakes each time a real request arrives. A scale lights up for one trillion and promptly dims.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Unlimited Tokens. Build-time A I helps make the app. Its meter belongs to whatever tool builds it. Production Workers A I runs when a live app needs it.

**Orson Quill**

A customer asks a question. A search returns a useful answer with sources. They come back tomorrow. Multiply that by useful apps people actually own.

**Mabel Finch**

And if the model adds nothing?

**Orson Quill**

Turn it off.

**Mabel Finch**

Unlimited.

**Orson Quill**

That's the folder name, Finch.

## Operation Self-Replication

INT. SCREENING WALL. A film frame travels through an optical relay into public Share. A different pair of hands puts a new variation on a pin board. Label any reuse example as a roadmap illustration.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Self-Replication. Share already lets people discover finished work. The ambition is a loop: communities ask for things, creators share what worked, and someone remixes what they're allowed to reuse.

**Mabel Finch**

With a credit for the original maker?

**Orson Quill**

In large, legible type. We have a reputation to ruin.

## Operation Sharks with Frickin’ Benchmarks

INT. TEST TANK. Rack focus from a fin behind glass to a comically small felt shark. Three score slips on a clipboard. Do not show invented performance numbers as evidence.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Sharks with Frickin' Benchmarks. Compare the same job. Show the output and the cost. Check what worked, what failed, and whether anyone comes back.

**Mabel Finch**

What happens when a result looks bad?

**Orson Quill**

We improve it or retire it.

**Mabel Finch**

Should I tell the shark?

**Orson Quill**

He's here for the control group.

## Operation Corporate Takeover

INT. SUBMERGED BOARDROOM. Three paperwork trays. Finch sets down a much smaller folder than Quill expected. Through the porthole, the shark turns the other way.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Corporate Takeover. Start with creators. Help agencies repeat what works for clients. Then prove enterprise use with a real sponsor and one bounded job.

**Orson Quill**

Roles, customer-controlled identity, approvals, costs and release history.

**Mabel Finch**

You're planning to win procurement?

**Orson Quill**

I prefer to think of it as getting invited back to procurement.

## Operation Ouroboros

INT. BRIEFING ROOM. Quill pulls the red lever. A miniature of the room slides out of a drawer. Mabel finds an ordinary cardboard moving box and takes the keys. End on a beat of silence.

**Orson Quill**

Operation Ouroboros. Rebuild App Composer using App Composer. In a separate owner's Cloudflare account. Restore the work, check what still runs and make the next change.

**Mabel Finch**

And if they can run it without us?

**Orson Quill**

That's the victory condition.

**Mabel Finch**

So your world domination plan is to make yourself optional.

**Orson Quill**

I was hoping you'd wait until after the credits.

**Mabel Finch**

I'll leave you a key.
