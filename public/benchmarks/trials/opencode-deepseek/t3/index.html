About

Night Shift Radio is a two-hour programme of quiet music and long pauses, recorded late at night on a kitchen table in a small flat. It is made by one person, with a borrowed microphone, a second-hand recorder, and a stubborn preference for leaving things unfinished rather than filling them.

Each episode keeps the room sounds in: the kettle, the chair, the bus that passes at ten past. Nothing is scripted and nothing is sponsored.

Guestbook

The guestbook is a plain notebook that travels with the show. Leave a note about where you listened, what the weather was doing, or the last quiet hour you can remember.

Nothing is published without your say-so, and nothing is ever sold.
