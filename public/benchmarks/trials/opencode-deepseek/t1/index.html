About

Night Shift Radio is a two-hour programme of slow instrumentals, field recordings and half-finished piano pieces. It is recorded after dark in a small kitchen, one take at a time, with the window open so the street gets into the mix.

There is no schedule and no sponsor. Episodes arrive when they are ready, usually a few times a season, and stay free to listen to.
