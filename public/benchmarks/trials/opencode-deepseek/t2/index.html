About

Night Shift Radio is a two-hour programme of slow instrumentals, field recordings and half-finished songs, assembled after hours at a kitchen table in a small flat. It is made by one person, on a laptop, with a borrowed microphone and a kettle that is always slightly too loud.

Each episode is built around a single mood rather than a tracklist. Nothing is rushed, nothing is mastered for volume, and the gaps between pieces are left in on purpose. It is meant to be played while you are doing something else.
