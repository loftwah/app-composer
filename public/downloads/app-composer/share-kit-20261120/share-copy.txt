LINKEDIN / FOUNDER POST

I keep coming back to one test for AI-built software. Open the same project next week and make a change that matters.

Can you find the source? Does the data stay yours? Can you ship the change without rebuilding the whole thing or losing the work around it?

That's the App Composer pitch. We put the full product vision online, then used it as the source for a short NotebookLM film, two discussions and a slide deck. One of the discussions takes the WordPress migration trade-offs seriously. The full vision links to the briefing, including transcripts and chapter links.
