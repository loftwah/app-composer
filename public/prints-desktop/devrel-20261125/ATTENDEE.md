# Your first run

**No account** — Open the [workshop](https://prints.loftwah.com/make), remix the editable brief and inspect the example. Run the included local Worker. A working result has a format, dimensions, a caption and zero inference calls.

**Local Oyster** — Follow the [Oyster quickstart](https://docs.fungi.computer/oyster-quickstart). The verified workshop pair is Oyster 0.59.0 and Node 24.20.0. Open Prints on your trial host. The library needs a selected Computer and folder. Local generation stays off.

**Owned cloud** — Use your own Cloudflare account. Choose either the standalone Worker and Gateway lab or a full [Oyster deployment](https://docs.fungi.computer/guides/run-your-own-oyster). The latter requires Workers Paid for its Container and the listed services. Model usage and provider access are funded separately. Fungi Hub access follows the [current hosted quickstart](https://docs.fungi.computer/quickstart).

**Verify one thing before building another** — Mark the local Worker complete after both responses. Mark Gateway complete after a real request is visible in your own Analytics or Logs. Mark Prints generation complete once the original appears in the chosen folder and can be exported. Keep these outcomes separate when showing your work.
