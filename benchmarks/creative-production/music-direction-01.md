## Question and source

Can a creator and their chosen agent turn an owned song, reference pack and
precise direction into a coherent editable film, then correct one shot without
losing accepted work?

The research source is [AirBuddDwyer's SlopCannon quickstart at
217fce0c](https://github.com/AirBuddDwyer/slopcannon-quickstart/tree/217fce0ceedbee88e1465fe16851ecf77bbee31a).
Its reported 230-second, 22-scene, 13-reference result and zero rerolls are
creator claims. This protocol does not reproduce or validate them. It uses
their production structure with original, permitted material. Provider credit
counts are not a currency comparison, and a successful first take excludes
neither reference preparation nor planning effort.

## Freeze the production contract

Record recording/lyric revisions and hashes, permitted reference versions,
brief, output aspect/resolution, product/source revisions, actual
agent/harness/model/adapter versions, time/spend/revision caps and reviewer
method. Instrumental audio is a valid control. Preserve manual lyric
corrections and unknown timing instead of inventing words.

The shared style describes the persistent visual treatment. Each shot retains:

- Stable ID and absolute source-audio start/end; word/phrase or beat cues with
  their method and confidence where available.
- Timed physical actions and expressions, intended starting and ending state.
- Allowed cast and outfit/identity variants, location, relative positions and
  locked screen sides; shot-local weather, time and props.
- Shot size, framing, angle, lens instruction, one camera movement or locked
  camera, and source/direction of light.
- Reference IDs, roles and versions: face anchor, costume, empty environment
  plate, staged starting frame or another relevant input.
- Selected/rejected takes, actual source trims, compiled-input identity and
  current saved project revision.

The storyboard exposes these facts with inspectable reference pixels. Record
**requested, supported and actually transmitted** inputs separately. A one-image
route receiving a staged frame is a distinct route, not proof of multiple
reference conditioning. Lens/camera wording is prompt guidance unless the
adapter exposes a genuine parameter. Reference/style edits identify affected
shots without regenerating them.

Missing detail can be suggested. Location plates, face anchors, naming rules
and reference counts remain task/adapter choices rather than mandatory forms
or claims that prompt wording guarantees compliance.

## Measurements and judgement

| Dimension | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Plan/input correctness | Project pack retained; shot subsets and compiled requests match saved intentions; timing coverage and declared tolerances checked; no unrelated cast/weather in requests. |
| Continuity/direction | Identity, costume, cast count, set geography, props, intended action, camera/light and ending assessed over complete takes. Correct requests do not prove model compliance. |
| Mouth/performer timing | On a route with verified audio conditioning, assess visible mouth/action timing against the selected vocal window. Prompt-only acting stays distinct; unsupported/instrumental cases receive no fabricated lip-sync score. |
| Final master sync | Inspect approved soundtrack, joins, offsets, duration and phrase-safe cuts in the actual export, accounting for encoding delay. A correct master does not establish mouth sync. |
| Craft | Brief fit, visual direction, musical emphasis, pacing, variation, narrative clarity and coherent finish with timecoded examples. |
| Human appeal | Authentic owner/independent reviewer preference and coverage; pending judgement stays pending. Technical checks or automated critics cannot supply human taste. |
| Revision/reliability | Restart/reconcile, correct one shot, reopen and re-export the same revision; verify unaffected accepted hashes and earlier masters. Distinguish browser encode restart from resumable encoding. |
| Cost/effort | Reference preparation/generation, planning/agent inference, failed/rejected takes, retries, critique, deterministic processing/render and human minutes. Keep quotes, estimates, observed charges, unknowns and credit use distinct. |

Review full-duration playback with sound for whole-film claims. Sampled frames
or transcripts support narrower observations only. Set task-specific timing
tolerances before a run and retain raw errors. For an instrumental control,
assess musical structure and action without hallucinated lyrics or mouth motion.

Report **first-take accepted scene slots / all attempted scene slots**, plus all
generated takes, failed submissions and accepted final slots. Retain every
attempt, including rejected takes. Separate first-pass and repaired outcomes.
Report accepted finished seconds and all attributable cost per accepted
finished second. With zero accepted seconds, show spend and an undefined ratio;
pending acceptance cannot be converted into an accepted-cost claim.

## Lean comparisons

Use #1019's matched-model and complete-product lanes. Matched-model trials
freeze accessible model/version, source, settings, output profile and effort;
disclose hidden prompt enhancement or inaccessible seeds. Product trials share
the brief and time/cost allowance while using each product's supported methods.

Choose one meaningful ablation in the first supported pilot:

- Generic action text versus timed action/camera/light direction.
- Costume-only input versus an inspected dedicated identity anchor.
- Character-only references versus inspected environment plates/staged frame.

Change one factor where supported, retain the baseline and count preparation
for both. Use the instrumental control to separate editing/master-sync from
singing. Review quality before revealing provider/price when practical, then
assess value. Record blinding limits, sample counts, ties and uncertainty.

Use retained clean/defective examples for critic checks: wrong cast, flipped
geography, background bleed, missing final action, a cut through a word, audio
offset and unjustified singing claims. Record false accepts/rejects and
inspected modalities; this does not commission an extra paid corpus.
One case cannot establish universal quality or audience performance.
