# Writing and interface standard
Reviewed 13 October 2026. MP requested this standard after the first launch copy repeated paired slogans and technical disclaimers.

Write the action and its result. “Feature an approved photo” is useful; “Independent pieces. Working together.” says less. Use actual product examples and source-backed claims. Vary sentence and section length to suit the subject. Avoid invented popularity, inflated importance, decorative triads, mirrored slogans, empty adjectives, forced contrasts and repeated reminders that something is “real”. Keep security limits in the relevant action or documentation, where they help a decision.

Use original functional graphics, clear hierarchy, restrained motion, readable contrast and native keyboard controls. Avoid an interchangeable tile grid, gratuitous gradients/glows, decorative terminal output, oversized slogan stacks and icons without meaning. Cloudflare-adjacent neutral surfaces and orange accents are welcome; its logo, claimed affiliation and exact dashboard reproduction are not our identity.

Dark mode is the default. Light mode is a saved reader choice. Theme affects presentation only, never credentials or deployment authority. Inspect both at phone and desktop widths before publishing a visual change. Preserve reduced-motion preferences.

AI assistance is part of the product, not a fact to conceal. Quality comes from specific writing, useful interaction and verified software; no detector score proves authorship or quality.

Minimum release standard applies to every project under the Woodhouse banner, as directed by MP on 13 October 2026. Existing owning issues retain gaps until addressed; partial implementation does not imply portfolio-wide compliance. Build keyboard paths and visible focus, mobile layouts and both themes, useful metadata and icons, original generated raster artwork when illustrations are needed, and repository contribution/security/accessibility guidance. Ctrl/Cmd+K opens a searchable app/task launcher; ordinary links and native controls remain usable without it. Public source includes a licence, approachable setup and focused test commands. Keep short development checks optional and targeted; production checks remain mandatory. Do not use a detector score as evidence of quality or authorship.

MP clarification, 16 October 2026: public text links must not be underlined, including hover and current-navigation states. Preserve visible keyboard focus and make links distinguishable through their context, weight, colour or control treatment. Sharing acceptance requires inspecting the actual app-specific image and copy at phone/feed size, checking live public crawler responses and recording any unverified platform rendering. Meta tags alone do not establish a good preview.

Public Copy/Share controls derive their URL only from published canonical/title/description/artwork metadata, remove unrelated query/fragment data and use a metadata revision query to give updated cards a new share URL. Keep artwork filenames versioned when their pixels change. Preserve the clean SEO canonical. Verify actual platform rendering and do not claim that a new URL purges earlier cached previews.

MP design clarification, 16 October 2026: an assembled set of generic components is below the intended quality bar. Establish a clear first action, deliberate hierarchy, coherent typography/spacing and real owner content. Keep utility controls visually quiet. The theme control shows the current icon with a stable accessible toggle name/state; it does not display the opposite mode as a prominent text label. Test actual phone/desktop interaction in both themes and judge the whole composition. More cards, animation, gradients or a detector score do not substitute for those decisions.
