# Curate a visual collection

[Open the collection shelf](https://composer.loftwah.com/collections). Every
collection opens with a lead image and a clear reason to explore. A first
edition usually contains three to five pieces. A pin opens with a close-up,
the reason it belongs, its source and one useful action.

## Choose the pieces

1. **Write the collection in one sentence.** Name what someone comes for,
   what makes the set distinct and what they can take away. Choose a lead
   image that communicates the idea at phone width.
2. **Make each pin earn its place.** Include a different treatment, object,
   view or useful step. Start with one strong lead, then add depth with
   something the reader can inspect, open, adapt or keep. Review candidate
   images side by side. Leave repeated crops and weaker alternatives in the
   working notes.
3. **Record origin and eligibility.** Keep the original or exact released
   source, capture date when relevant, who owns the work, permitted use and
   the specific quality being carried forward. Link to the source in the
   pin. The original and its terms stay with the owning project. Obtain the
   artwork or publication approval required by that project before promotion.
4. **Finish the thumbnail and close-up together.** Inspect the real pixels
   at 390px and 1440px in both themes. Check the main subject, lettering,
   contrast, edge crops and coverage by the Save control. Use a separate
   thumbnail when a full image or small text would lose the point. Version
   new exports and retain existing published originals.
5. **Use the finished board.** Open each pin. Follow its action and source.
   Check Escape, focus return, Save, Saved and reload. Verify the deployed
   source and the same journey, then record the release and any remaining
   limits in the owning issue.

## Add an edition

Add the collection and its pins to [the catalogue](../src/lib/visual-collections.ts).
Use stable collection and pin IDs so existing browser saves follow the piece.
Provide the lead and pin dimensions, meaningful alt text, source link and note,
and the primary action. Keep private or speculative material in the owning
project’s existing workspace. Publish the finished edition when the pieces
are ready, with an entry on the shelf and a link from the relevant product
page. Add the public route to [the indexable inventory](../src/lib/public-pages.mjs).
