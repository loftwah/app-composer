# Visual direction

Owner direction, 18 October 2026. This supplements DESIGN.md and the source/asset maintenance contract. Read it before proposing, generating or changing artwork, identity marks, icons or social cards. It applies to App Composer and its five independent mini-apps. Each app retains its own committed assets and release.

## What the work needs to express

Useful software with an authored world around it: inventive machinery, strange inhabitants, music and game culture, character and humour. Things have a job. A joke can come from what a character does, what a machine gets wrong, or a small consequence in the scene. Smiling at the viewer with an arbitrary prop does not establish a character.

Dean's public work combines software construction, music production and playable games. His original Linkarooie character and public cartoon portrait are actual identity references. Use these concrete sources rather than deriving a generic persona from a job title. Personal portrait, biography and banner belong to Linkarooie/owner surfaces; the Composer product remains its own product.

The desired animation language can draw on adult science-fiction cartoons, including the owner's Rick and Morty reference: expressive ink contours, deliberate awkwardness, readable flat forms, purposeful exaggeration and dry humour. Create original characters and objects. A familiar cartoon style is a reference for craft, not a substitute for an original idea.

## Drawing and composition

- Choose an idea specific to the app before describing a style. Explain what the subject is doing and why it belongs to that software.
- Give each app a distinct silhouette and focal action within the same visual world. A row of interchangeable smiling heads is insufficient.
- Use confident drawn contours, clear shapes and controlled flat/cel colour. Keep the deliberate quirks that make a figure recognisable.
- Use camera, gesture, proportion and negative space deliberately. Add a detail only when it contributes character, meaning or an intentional visual joke.
- Design an optical small-size variant where needed. A full scene shrunk to 32 pixels is not an icon system.
- Match hero, icon, catalogue and social derivatives through the selected identity. Do not quietly mix rejected characters, obsolete marks or unrelated generated styles.

Do not default to glossy sports/team mascots, children's sticker packs, rounded toy robots, huge sparkling eyes, arbitrary baseball caps, generic chain links or cubes, decorative planet cards, gradients/glows as polish, stock clip art, corporate flat illustration, or ornamental sci-fi clutter. The owner has explicitly rejected these substitutions. A material may be glossy when the subject calls for it; the praised Bubbles card is a composition/finish benchmark, not a directive to make every character shiny.

## Judge the delivered use

### The Bubbles process is the directing benchmark

MP explicitly reaffirmed the current Bubbles card on 18 October: learn how it was directed, not its appearance. The actual [source and evidence](https://github.com/loftwah/bubbles/tree/455f9d56b9feda26d06aa1ad78838797184c4453/evidence/issue-1326) establish the useful method:

1. Lock an existing recognisable identity. Bubbles used its shipped Bubblecap character, with explicit anatomy invariants; it did not invent a new mascot for the card.
2. Stage one meaningful moment. A blue shot makes a match; the character reacts. The scene communicates the game's appeal without pretending to be a screenshot.
3. Direct camera, focal point, motion and negative space for the specific format. A wide social card and a tiny icon need different compositions.
4. Generate illustration without lettering. Compose the real product wordmark, existing copy and actual font in a reproducible renderer after the art exists.
5. Compare editorial treatments at full size, 600px and 300px. Use the same master to distinguish composition/copy choices from an entirely different art concept.
6. Inspect and state crop limits. The Bubbles work explicitly exposed square-crop clipping rather than calling one wide image a universal icon.
7. Select through visual judgement, then complete provenance, cache/version changes, all consumers and live pixel verification.

Do not reduce this method to “make a cute character,” “use more neon,” or another long list of style adjectives. Identity, an idea, staging, typography and the final use have to work together.

Inspect decoded/exported pixels after fonts and images load. Review the actual header, launcher, catalogue, browser tab, installed icon, public page and social card in both themes, on a phone and desktop. Check 16/32/48/96px identity, 180/192/512px install crops and the real 1200×630 sharing presentation. Important subjects and copy must remain legible at thumbnail size.

All six repositories need their own reviewed output. Standalone and mounted routes, owner/editor surfaces, cached URLs, HTML metadata and catalogue copies must agree. Keep old immutable URLs intact; changed bytes receive a new versioned name and every active consumer is updated. An HTTP 200 or matching hash proves delivery, not artistic quality.

Agents own routine art-direction and implementation decisions within this brief. Generated output is a candidate. Displaying it, passing a checker or saying it is reviewed does not make it owner-approved. Preserve useful source/provenance and rejected-direction notes so the next run does not restart the same failed exploration.
