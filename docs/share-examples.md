# Publish an example in Share

[Finished projects](https://composer.loftwah.com/demo/examples) gives visitors
a finished result they can inspect, open or adapt. Link there from the product
page. Use the existing Artwork, Films or Creator stacks section when the work
already fits one of those.

Add the example to [the Share catalogue](../src/lib/share-examples.ts). Give it a
clear description and choose three to five pieces that each show something
different. Link directly to the working tool, editable sample or finished export.

Record who owns each piece, its usage terms, its original source and why it was
selected. Keep the original with the owning project and follow its publication
approval. Keep private or exploratory work in the owning project's workspace.

Use the actual output or a capture of the working tool. Inspect the real pixels
at phone and desktop widths in both themes. Check that the image, source and
action work on the deployed Share page. See [visual direction](visual-direction.md)
for identity and artwork requirements.
