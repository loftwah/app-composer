## 1 The short versions

### TLDR in one sentence

App Composer helps people and their chosen AI agents turn an idea into a useful project they own, give it a polished public presence, and keep improving it with sound engineering underneath.

### TLDR in thirty seconds

Imagine describing an application, website, game or creative project, showing a few examples of what you like, and receiving something you can actually use. App Composer connects the work needed to get there: planning, software, design, media, deployment, publishing and ongoing operation. You can stay in a guided experience or inspect the source and infrastructure whenever you want. The result belongs to you, including the material and project history needed to change it later. Cloudflare provides the first production foundation; your agent and the connected applications do the appropriate work. [P01](#source-p01) [P02](#source-p02)

### TLDR for a technical reader

App Composer is an open-source creation and operations platform built around independently owned applications and explicit service boundaries. Its Astro and TypeScript application uses EmDash for native administration and content workflows. Cloudflare Workers is the primary deployment target, with storage, identity, AI and coordination services selected for the workload. GitHub retains source and change history. Supported external agents implement authorised work through scoped integrations, while Composer retains the brief, project relationships, reusable capability contracts and a coherent view of results. Media Studio owns creative production and its asset lifecycle; connected apps own their own records, permissions and releases. [P01](#source-p01) [P03](#source-p03)

The engineering proposition is continuity across the full lifecycle. A generated preview becomes an owned deployment; a requested change preserves existing content and customisation; an asset retains its source and rights through a derivative; an interrupted job can be reconciled; a release can be tied to the exact source that produced it. The system exposes useful operations to humans and agents without making Composer a compulsory runtime dependency for every app.

### TLDR for someone who does not work in technology

You should be able to make a useful thing without first learning how to run an internet company. App Composer gives you a place to explain the idea, make important choices, see the result and change your mind. It helps with the work that usually surrounds the idea: setting up the application, making it look good, preparing images and videos, putting it online, sharing it and looking after it.

You might use it to launch a personal website, a small business tool, an interactive game, a music project or an automated research service. You can use an existing example as a starting point, combine useful pieces, or ask an agent to create a missing feature. When you return, your project is still there with its previous decisions and editable work. You do not have to begin the explanation again.

### The executive summary

The central customer problem is the amount of disconnected work between having an idea and owning a good result. Software creation is only part of that work. People also need design, content, media, accounts, deployment, updates, distribution, support and some understanding of cost. Each handoff can lose context or leave a task unfinished.

App Composer carries one project through those handoffs. Its value comes from making the next useful action clear and connecting it to the right application, agent or service. A beginner sees an understandable workflow. An engineer sees the underlying contracts and evidence. Both work on the same project.

A customer's application does not require the founder's private accounts or the Woodhouse installation to remain useful. It can continue under its owner's control, with its own source, assets and service connections. [P03](#source-p03) [P06](#source-p06)

The surrounding portfolio gives the platform practical substance. Paperwork handles real PDFs. LDraw provides an editable diagramming workspace. Linkarooie and Here / there demonstrate connected creator applications. Media Studio produces artwork, audio, films and reusable assets. LoftwahFM supplies an independent music platform. Games such as Fighter, SHOALSHOT, Bubbles and Protocol 11 exercise interaction, performance, persistence and presentation in ways a conventional business form cannot. These products remain useful in their own right while contributing tested methods and components to the wider system. [P01](#source-p01) [P20](#source-p20) [P21](#source-p21)

The mission has two related parts. For the customer, it is easier creation and continued ownership of useful work. For the Cloudflare ecosystem, it is more people building on Workers and using Workers AI for tasks that are worth doing. Useful repeat use connects those missions. Increasing token consumption without improving an outcome would miss the customer purpose. [P02](#source-p02) [P22](#source-p22)

The defining acceptance test is demanding: a person gives the agent an idea, the system delivers a useful mini-app and its launch material, authorised publication actually happens, the project continues operating, and the owner is proud to share the result without repairing it themselves. Technical checks, creative judgement and observed operation all contribute to that result. [P23](#source-p23)
